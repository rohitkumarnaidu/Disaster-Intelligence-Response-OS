---
name: Demo boundary
description: Durable product boundary for the DRAXELYRA historical replay.
---

DRAXELYRA's initial experience is a deterministic, historical/demo-only urban flood replay modeled loosely on Chennai. It must never imply that seeded imagery, detections, or response state are live emergency intelligence.

**Why:** The product is intended to demonstrate the evidence-to-action workflow without overstating operational readiness or access to institutional feeds.

**How to apply:** Preserve clear DEMO REPLAY, MOCK INFERENCE, and historical/source labels wherever seeded detections, imagery, metrics, or task state are shown.
